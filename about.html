The studio

Design is a labor of love—just like a great kitchen.

D Philhower Studio builds brands that feel personal, polished, and ready for the real world.

Led by David Philhower, the studio blends classic layout craft with modern digital polish. Every project starts with a conversation—what you serve, who you serve, and how you want people to feel when they meet your brand.

We specialize in work for restaurants and small businesses: logos that live on aprons and apps, posters that fill windows, print that holds up under coffee cups, and websites that bring the dining room online.

Based in New Jersey. Working with clients who care about the details.
