How to play

  • Keyboard: use A/D or arrow keys to steer.
  • Touch: hold Left or Right.
  • Collect coupon tickets to recharge your boost.
  • Avoid wet-floor spots, cereal displays, and rival carts.

Game pitch

A lighthearted shopping-cart racer built for mobile web and iOS WebView play. The theme is store chaos, not targeting people: the characters are scrappy carts with personality, racing for fun.
