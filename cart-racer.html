Store Dash Grand Prix

Steer through the aisles, collect care kits, bump through boost pads, and finish three laps ahead of the rival carts.

Controls

Use arrow keys or A/D on desktop. On iOS, hold the left and right buttons or drag across the canvas.

Pickups

Care kits add points, boost pads give a burst of speed, and aisle arrows help you recover after a hit.

Hazards

Avoid mop puddles, cereal spills, loose crates, and roaming rival carts while the store timer ticks down.
