Ready to roll?

Steer the cart between grocery aisles, collect coupons for boosts, and avoid spills, cereal towers, and rival carts.

Touch controls

Tap the arrows, swipe left or right on the track, and press Boost when a coupon is loaded.

Keyboard controls

Use arrow keys or A/D to steer. Press Space to spend a coupon boost.

Goal

Finish three laps with the highest score and the best place at checkout.
