After-hours race

Ready to roll?

Steer through three laps of the supermarket. Pick up donation groceries for points and use items to pass the rival carts.

Controls

On iPhone or iPad, hold the on-screen arrows to steer. Tap Push for a quick sprint, Brake for tight turns, and Item when you have a pickup.

Keyboard: arrow keys or A/D to steer, W to push, S to brake, and E or Space to use an item.

Pickups

Coupon boosts launch your cart forward. Grocery boxes add to the donation score. Mystery totes can become a shield, a sprint, or a small burst that helps you overtake nearby carts.

Hazards

Mop spills and display stacks slow the cart down unless a shield is active. Rival carts weave across the aisle, so time your push before corners and pickup rows.
