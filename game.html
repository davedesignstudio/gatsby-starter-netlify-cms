Original iOS-friendly store racer

Cart Dash: After-Hours Aisle Run

Race a scrappy shopping cart through a closed superstore, grab coupon boosts, dodge spills and displays, and finish every department checklist before the night crew locks the doors.

Ready for the aisle run?

Steer the cart, collect coupon boosts, avoid messy hazards, and keep your combo alive across three store departments.

How to play

  • Touch Left / Right, use arrow keys, or drag on the canvas to steer.
  • Collect golden coupons to charge boosts.
  • Tap Boost or press Space for a quick sprint.
  • Avoid spills, fallen displays, and rival carts.

Race status

Goal: Finish Produce, Bakery, and Frozen.

Tip: Clean driving keeps your speed multiplier high.
