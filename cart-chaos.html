How it plays

  • Auto-accelerating arcade racer inspired by kart games.
  • Built for touch screens and keyboard play.
  • Three laps, rival carts, boosts, spills, and shelf collisions.

Controls

  • Touch: hold left or right, brake, or turbo.
  • Keyboard: A / D or arrow keys to steer.
  • Brake: S, down arrow, or space bar.
  • Turbo: W, up arrow, or shift.

Arcade handling

The player cart auto-accelerates like a casual mobile racer while steering stays snappy enough to thread through endcaps and shelf turns.

Store hazards

Watch for juice spills, loose produce, and stacked shelf islands that can wipe speed off your cart if you clip them.
