iPhone-first racing prototype

Cart Chaos: a supermarket kart racer with wild shopping-cart energy.

This playable concept turns the repo into a fast, touch-friendly arcade racer where battered carts drift through aisles, dodge cleanup spills, collect canned-energy boosts, and sprint for the checkout line.

Play the vertical slice

Race three rival carts for three laps. Best experienced in landscape on a phone, but keyboard controls work too.

Why it feels like kart racing

  • Three rival AI carts chase a snaking aisle circuit inside a big-box store.
  • Boost pickups and slippery cleanup spills create the swingy arcade rhythm you want from a kart racer.
  • A live minimap, lap tracking, place ranking, and punchy collisions make it readable on small screens.

Controls

  • W / Up accelerate
  • A / Left steer left
  • D / Right steer right
  • S / Down brake or reverse
  • Shift or Space boost
  • R quick restart

The implementation is a browser-based vertical slice inside this repo, which is the fastest way to prove out the iOS game loop, touch controls, and art direction without an existing Xcode project.
