# Cart Clash

Mario Kart–style racing with **stray shopping carts** tearing through a midnight supermarket.

Play on iPhone Safari (Add to Home Screen for app-like fullscreen) or any modern desktop browser.

## Play

Open `cart-clash/index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
npx --yes serve cart-clash
```

Then visit the local URL on your phone (same Wi‑Fi) for the full touch experience.

## Controls

| Action | Touch | Keyboard |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Steer  | ◀ ▶   | ← → / A D |
| Brake  | BRAKE | Space / S |
| Item   | ITEM  | E / Z / X |

## Gameplay

- Pick a cart (Rusty, Squeaky, Bent Wheel, Coupon King)
- Race **3 laps** around the SuperSaver aisle loop
- Grab glowing pickups for bananas, soup cans, bag boosts, and spilled oil
- Beat three rival carts: Dumpster Duo, Parking Lot Pete, Rain Cap Rita

## iOS tips

- Use **Safari** → Share → **Add to Home Screen**
- Landscape or portrait both work; portrait is tuned for thumbs
- Keep the phone awake — races are short

## Tech

Pure HTML5 Canvas + ES modules. No build step. Mode‑7 style floor projection, AI racers, and item combat.
