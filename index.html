How the prototype plays

The karts follow a supermarket loop, but lane control still matters. Swing wide into the frozen aisle, cut back through the produce corner, and hit pickup crates to arm a new item.

  • Gas: hold W or Up Arrow, or press the Gas button.
  • Steer: use A/D or Left/Right Arrow to shift your lane and line.
  • Brake: use S or Down Arrow to tighten up your turn.
  • Use item: press Space, Enter, or the Use Item button.

Item set

  • Turbo Coffee: a burst of straight-line speed.
  • Soup Lid Shield: blocks one hazard hit.
  • Soda Spill: leaves a slick trap behind your cart.

AI rivals will chase pickups too, so the race changes every lap instead of feeling scripted.

Why this feels like a compact iOS game prototype

The page is built around a single focused loop: accelerate, steer for clean lines, grab items, and finish first. It is structured like a mobile arcade pitch instead of a generic website landing page.

Touch-first controls

The on-screen buttons make the game immediately playable on phones and tablets without extra setup.

Readable combat items

Boosts, shields, and spills give the race a Mario Kart-style rhythm without overwhelming the player.

Theme built into the track

Produce shelves, freezer lanes, and checkout hazards sell the cart-racing-in-store fantasy from the first lap.
