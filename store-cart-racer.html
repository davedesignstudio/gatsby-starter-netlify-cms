iPhone and iPad-friendly prototype

Store Cart Sprint

Race souped-up shopping carts through a late-night superstore, dodge endcaps, scoop up battery boosts, and fight for first across three neon-lit laps.

Launch race Gameplay notes
  • 6th Current place
  • 1 / 3 Lap
  • 70% Boost meter
  • 75.0s Race clock

Ready for the night shift?

Swipe or tap left and right to switch aisles. Hold boost when your meter is charged and finish the three-lap run ahead of the other carts.

Keyboard support: A / D or arrow keys to steer, space to boost.

Arcade loop

This prototype is tuned for mobile touch screens, but it keeps the arcade energy of a kart racer: rivals push pace over three laps, pickups refill your battery meter, and collisions with displays or floor signs drain momentum until you recover.

How to play

  • Move across four aisles to line up clean passes.
  • Grab battery packs for extra boost energy.
  • Avoid checkout signs, displays, and produce spills.

What feels kart-like

  • Three-lap structure with live race position tracking.
  • Boost management that rewards timing instead of button mashing.
  • Distinct rivals with pace variance and lane changes.

iOS direction

The game runs as a browser prototype today, and its systems map cleanly to a native iOS build in SpriteKit or Unity if you want to keep expanding the concept.
