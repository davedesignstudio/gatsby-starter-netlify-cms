Ready to roll?

Steer through the grocery aisles, collect supply packs, and use coupons for short boosts.

How to play

  • On iPhone or iPad, hold the left and right buttons or drag across the track to steer.
  • On keyboard, use arrow keys or A/D. Press Space for a coupon boost.
  • Collect supply packs for the shelter run and coupons for extra speed.
  • Avoid cereal spills, wet-floor signs, checkout cones, and rival cart bumps.

Prototype notes

This is a lightweight HTML5 game designed for iOS Safari: no app install, no dependencies, and no external assets. It can later be wrapped in a native iOS shell if needed.
